# 🏥 Smart Healthcare Risk Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The growing prevalence of chronic diseases such as Heart Disease, Diabetes, and Stroke creates a major challenge for healthcare providers. Early identification of high-risk patients can help healthcare professionals provide timely intervention and improve preventive care.

This project uses healthcare patient data and Tableau dashboards to analyze demographic information, clinical indicators, lifestyle behaviors, and symptoms associated with chronic diseases.

The analysis focuses on factors such as:

- Age
- BMI
- Blood Pressure
- Cholesterol
- Glucose Levels
- Smoking Habits
- Alcohol Consumption
- Exercise Levels
- Fatigue
- Chest Pain
- Dizziness
- Heart Disease
- Diabetes
- Stroke

The Tableau dashboards provide interactive visualizations that help identify disease patterns, compare risk factors, and understand vulnerable patient groups.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Identify patients with higher health-risk indicators.
2. Analyze the prevalence of Heart Disease, Diabetes, and Stroke.
3. Study the relationship between demographic factors and diseases.
4. Analyze the effect of lifestyle behaviors on disease occurrence.
5. Compare disease prevalence across different age groups.
6. Analyze disease patterns across BMI categories.
7. Identify important clinical and behavioral risk factors.
8. Support preventive healthcare and clinical decision-making through data visualization.

## 👨‍⚕️ Project Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Clinical Risk Assessment Officer

A clinical risk assessment officer needs to identify high-risk patients at an early stage. Tableau helps analyze BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, symptoms, and disease indicators to support timely clinical intervention.

### Scenario 2: Healthcare Data Analyst

A healthcare data analyst needs to understand how lifestyle behaviors influence disease occurrence. Tableau visualizations can reveal patterns involving smoking, alcohol consumption, and exercise levels.

### Scenario 3: Public Health Researcher

A public health researcher needs to understand population-wide disease patterns. Tableau helps compare disease prevalence across age groups, BMI categories, lifestyle behaviors, and symptoms.
